# Synthetic portfolio corpus v1

Status: local RI14 implementation; coordinator contract review pending. Owner: codex-portfolio-generator. Pricing schema: Alex `2df78cb08f782010f61acffc07d78030580e7639`, `engine.api.market_schemas.MarketPortfolioRequestSchema`. Its historical `schema_version="2"` is a field name, not an independently versioned API.

Each `portfolio-NNNN.json` is the strict base market/trades/run-configuration body. Bills and notes have `trade_type="bond"`, currency USD, signed `face_amount` (no second direction sign), redemption1, maturity after market.asof, and decimal coupon rate. Bills have zero coupons and no schedule. Notes have complete contiguous unadjusted regular six-month periods ending at maturity, payment on period end, ACT/ACT(ICMA). Curves use continuous ACT/365 zero rates at `[0,1,5,10]` year pillars; every rate is an explicitly assumed synthetic constant. No external observations/FX/credit/spread data are represented. NPV is dirty in USD. No settlement adjustment or exporter accrued fraction is claimed.

The sidecar `manifest.json` contains version, seed/settings, generator/engine/schema revision, deterministic corpus/portfolio/submission IDs, request paths/raw SHA256/byte lengths, expected supported/refused coverage and counts/net/gross signed face/coupon-period aggregates. IDs derive from settings hash plus portfolio/trade indices. Identical settings intentionally reproduce identities; choose a new seed for a separate workload. Signed net face is an accounting statistic, not risk/netting validation. Portfolio IDs are not sent as unknown engine body fields. Manifest bytes are deterministic too; no wall-clock timestamp contaminates reproducibility. Request hash is raw-byte identity; RI16 service identity additionally canonicalizes validated body/defaults and binds service revision.

RI16 additive transport contract `ri16-v1`: optional `Idempotency-Key` on POST `/portfolio/price`; GET `/portfolio/submissions/{id}` recovers job identity;202 may report worker unavailable after durable acceptance. Base service without RI16 can ignore that header and lacks lookup; its legacy POST remains unsafe to retry. This client submits once in either case. Poll `/portfolio/price/{job_id}` states pending/running/done/failed/interrupted;404 or unexpected replies remain unknown. Interrupted completion is unknown and never automatically rerun. The client does not promise cancellation, readiness, exactly-once execution, or safe multi-host SQLite use.

Small golden fixture: bills +/-100000 at2027-01-15 and4pct notes +/-100000 with2025-10-15/2026-04-15/2026-10-15 coupon boundaries, asof2026-01-15, flat3pct continuous curve. Independent reference directly enumerates cashflows; signed accrued =2000*92/182. This restricted reference does not copy engine curve interpolation, schedules, Monte Carlo or Greeks.
