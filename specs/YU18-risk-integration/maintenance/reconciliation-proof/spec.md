# Reconciliation proof requirements

Status: source implementation and offline acceptance complete; review pending. Component owner: codex-recon-proof. Depends on the existing YU05 proof and YU18 managed identity/checkpoint contracts.

- **FR-RP01 — Positive subject identity.** Read a successful JSON live run descriptor and forward window, and a successful SQL active-run join. Require agreement on scope, epoch, scheme and descriptor hash. Search forward pages within a bounded budget; require a SQL row with identical id, account, security, side, quantity and numeric price, plus matching provenance and positive consensus sequence. Unknown legacy scope, foreign identities, unreadable SQL/HTTP and empty/no-intersection windows refuse without planting a mismatch.
- **FR-RP02 — Exact mutation.** Re-read the subject after pausing the sole processor replica. Change only quantity by one with predicates for its full identity and original quantity; verify the applied value. Refuse integer overflow, evicted subjects, changed identity or no-op writes.
- **FR-RP03 — Fresh subject classification.** Save/reset the selected scope's persisted reconciliation checkpoint while no processor pod remains. Start a distinct ready pod and own a forward to that exact pod. Require a readable status with a sweep witness and cursor reaching the subject, a positive mismatch count, and an exact id token in that pod's logs. Another id, a substring match, old pod identity or stale/incomplete cursor cannot satisfy the control. Restore quantity and reclassify from zero in another distinct pod; require zero mismatch and no subject mismatch log.
- **FR-RP04 — Restoration.** On success or catchable EXIT/INT/TERM/HUP, terminate the owned forward, stop the processor, restore and verify original quantity and checkpoint, and restore its original one-replica setting. Do not touch other checkpoint scopes or unrelated trade rows. A restoration failure fails loudly. SIGKILL, host loss and unavailable remote services cannot promise restoration; preserve the diagnostic and investigate before reuse.
- **FR-RP05 — Existing proof arms.** Preserve full-history live-counter bracketing, cross-member sequence/trade bracketing, and orphan absent/named/removed assertions. Own a forward to the current ready processor pod without changing its replica setting, rather than reuse the tunnel invalidated by the subject control. Plant a uniquely attributed orphan in the originally observed managed SQL scope with an atomic registration predicate and require one affected row. Reconcile ambiguous committed insertion by its private nonce and complete expected row; never reissue INSERT. Preserve foreign rows following failed/no-op insertion. Delete with all original fields/provenance/nonce and byte-exact text guards; a changed/replaced row must be retained with an explicit cleanup refusal. Keep exact absent/named/removed membership assertions and qualified cleanup on INT/TERM/HUP.

Interfaces: admin `/run/status`, `/recon/trades/blotter`, `/recon/status`; Kubernetes deployment/pods/logs/scale/wait/forward; SQL `trades`, `projection_runs`, `projection_active`, `projection_recon`. No new production API. Direct execution remains a mutating proof requiring authorization for its selected rig; offline acceptance grants no retained-rig operation.

Acceptance uses independent fixture classification against SQLite rows through the actual subprocess path: older SQL plus rolled window, same-window and exact fresh identity, missing intersection, foreign run/projection, wrong field identity, failed/non-JSON reads, wrong mismatch identity, incomplete status/cursor, failed/no-op mutation and catchable interruption. Every case compares original versus final complete fixture tables and replica count.

R1 acceptance additionally executes writer insertion after the precheck, failed/no-op and committed-then-error INSERT, scope changes before/after insertion, changed ownership/fields (including case-only changes under a case-insensitive fixture collation), replacement between ownership read and DELETE, wrong/stale orphan identities, and helper/Bash signals during orphan mutation and sweep. External fixture rows and active-scope changes remain intact; successful and ambiguous owned inserts leave no residue.
